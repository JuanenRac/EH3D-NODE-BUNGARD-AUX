# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

- **The validation of the CI is the reusable one of the documentation hub:** the job `validate` calls `validate.yml` of EH3D-DOCS (pinned by commit) and what the repository checks besides it is in jobs of their own, with the check of the CHANGELOG.

## [0.0.9] - Why the protect output opened, stuck-task guard, board mark and core dump

- **The status page tells why the protect output is open now:** next to the number of times it has opened and the last cause, the page shows the reason that holds it open at this moment (speed over the limit, spindle too hot, temperature sensor not answering), in the 37 languages. `tests/check_panel_text.py` reads the reasons from `core/spindle.hpp` and fails if one has no text in the panel.

- **The ONVIF discovery task has a bigger stack:** the build's stack report showed its main function using 2480 bytes of the 4096 it had; it now has 6144.

- **The panel tells why the protect output opened:** under the protection card the status page says how many times it has opened since the start and the last cause (speed over the limit, spindle too hot, temperature sensor not answering), in the 37 languages of the panel.

- **The build in GitHub Actions lists the biggest stack frames:** with `EH3D_STACK_REPORT=1` (set only by that build) the compiler writes the stack use of every function and the build prints the twenty biggest of `main/` and `core/` in its summary, with a warning on any above 2048 bytes; the image does not change.

- **The settings header no longer carries the name of a machine:** `core/bungard_config.hpp` is now `core/node_config.hpp`, the header the shared firmware base includes, and the base and the copies in the nodes are kept equal by `tools/sync_firmware_base.py`.

- **The host tests also run under AddressSanitizer and UBSan in the CI:** the same programs are built with `-fsanitize=address,undefined` and run (an undefined behaviour stops the test), and `test.sh` / `test.bat` run the same steps as the CI (the repository checks, the tests of the tools, the pictures and the languages, and the host tests).

- **A task that is there but stuck is told:** the tasks that must keep moving call `nodeguard::beat(name)` each time round their loop, and the guard (`find_stalled` in `core/node_guard.hpp`) treats a task whose count has not changed for 30 seconds like a missing one: it is told in the log (`guard: task X stalled`) and, after a minute, the node restarts once, cleanly, under the same limit of three an hour.
- **A new image is confirmed only when what drives the machine has started:** the subsystems each node needs (`kSub*` bits in `core/node_guard.hpp`) report themselves up when they start, and the confirmation task goes back to the previous image, naming what is missing, when one of them did not start.
- **An image made for the other board is refused:** every image carries `EH3D-BOARD-MARK:<board>`; after `esp_ota_end` the node reads the written image back (`image_board_ok`, `core/board_mark.hpp`) and refuses one that names another board, from the upload and from GitHub, with the answer `wrong_firmware`. An image made before the mark existed is still accepted.
- **A crash leaves a core dump:** `sdkconfig.defaults` turns on the core dump to the existing partition and the log says at the next start that one is there.

- **Why the protect output is open:** the status says `protect_reason` (`too_fast`, `hot`, `sensor_silent` or `none`), `protect_trips` (how many times it has opened since the start), `protect_last` and `protect_last_s` (why and when it opened last); the rules are in `core/spindle.hpp` and tested on a computer, and the simulated head tells the same.

## [0.0.8] - Self-check of the head, simulated drill head with a tested contract and every setting written to flash

- **Settings that were forgotten at the next start:** the `head` section that is written to flash, shown to the panel and compared to decide whether a restart is needed left out the picture of the camera (mirror, flip, brightness, contrast, saturation, exposure, white balance), the input, edge and thresholds of the speed sensor, and the resolution and the address of the DS18B20. A change to any of them was applied to the running node, lost at the next start, and never asked for a restart. All of them are written now. A host test (`tests/test_head_roundtrip.cpp`) reads every key the settings parser accepts and fails when one is missing from the written document, and takes a document with every key changed through flash and back.
- **`head.cfg.example` lists every key, and is the defaults:** the laser (distance mode, timing budget, receiving area, outliers, calibration) and the ring of LEDs were missing; the host test fails for a key the parser knows and the example does not, for a key twice or unknown, and when the example read as settings is not the defaults.
- **The node judges itself:** `core/self_check.hpp` turns the status the node already builds and its settings into a verdict (`selfcheck`: `ok`, `warn`, `fail` or `off`) and the stable codes behind it (`selfcheck_codes`, `AUX-C01` to `AUX-S03`: the camera that did not start, a laser fault or an old or weak reading, noisy speed pulses or a speed above `rpm_max`, a temperature sensor that is silent or a spindle that is warm or hot, the protect output off or open, a ring of LEDs that was not found, no token, no stream password). Both keys are in `/api/status`. The limits are settings in `head.cfg` (`selfcheck_enabled`, `selfcheck_laser_age_max_s`, `selfcheck_grace_s`), the verdict never touches the hardware, and a reading that has not arrived yet in the first seconds after the start is not a fault. Tested on a computer; not run on a board.
- **A head that is not there:** `core/head_sim.hpp` is a simulated drill head (a table that is not flat under a laser, a spindle that speeds up and heats, the ring of LEDs, the camera) that builds the status and the answer to a measure through the same code as the firmware, with faults that can be switched on (camera down, laser fault or weak signal, noisy pulses, missing temperature reading, ring not found). `tests/head_sim_cli.cpp` answers request lines on the standard input for a program that wants to read a head that is not there. The numbers of the table, the noise and the heating are made up; nothing here is a measurement of a machine.
- **The contract with the programs is written down and tested:** `docs/CONTRACT.md` lists every key of `/api/status` and of the answer to a measure, which ones the server cannot do without, and every self-check code with its severity; `tests/test_head_sim.cpp` reads that page and fails when a key or a code is on one side and not on the other.
- **The self-test reads the verdict:** `tools/selftest.py` has a step that shows the node's own check and fails it when the node says `fail`.

## [0.0.7] - Shared firmware base and checks brought up to date

- **Brand:** Add the application icon and the logos of the program.
- **Shared checks updated:** the validator also compares the badge and the Version row of the README with the manifest, fails when one of its checks cannot run, and the CHANGELOG step of the workflow reads the base branch from the environment.
- **A watchdog for the manual build:** `.github/workflows/build-watchdog.yml` looks every hour for a build that stays queued or running longer than the limit in `.github/build-watchdog.json` and opens (or updates) an issue `build-stuck`, which it closes when no build is stuck.
- **Firmware builds in GitHub Actions:** `.github/workflows/firmware-build.yml` (manual, owner only) builds the images in the pinned ESP-IDF container, writes `SHA256SUMS`, a build manifest and a provenance attestation, checks the version inside every image, opens an issue `build-failed` when it fails and prepares an unsigned draft release only after the owner approves it; the signature stays on the maintainer's computer. See `docs/BUILD.md`.

- **A self-test for the day the head meets the hardware:** `tools/selftest.py` reads the node (version, state, temperature, rpm, light state, a camera picture, the stream, the RTSP answer, one laser reading, the settings export compared with itself and never applied, backup networks, OTA state) and writes `selftest_aux_<time>.txt` with one line per step. With `--active` and a typed confirmation (or `--yes`) it switches the light ring on for two seconds and puts it back, checking the result even when a step fails. It never runs the laser calibration; nothing is written without `--active`; the tokens and the password are never printed. Its tests run against a toy node in the CI.
- **`docs/HARDWARE_CHECKLIST.md`:** the steps to do by hand on the bench, with what must happen and where to write the result.
- **Issue forms and a pull-request template:** the repository now has the bug report (it asks for the versions that `eh3d-updater --cli status --offline` lists), documentation, improvement and hardware validation forms (with the machine used) and the pull-request template of the suite.

- **Three checks that nothing ran are removed from `tools/`:** `_doc_policy.py`, `_readme_parity.py` and `_tracked_names.py` were copies of the checks of another family that no workflow, script or document named, and their header said another family kept them in step. The checks that run are in `tools/ci_validate.py`.
- **The workflows are hardened:** every action is pinned by its commit with its version in a comment, and the checkouts keep no credentials.
- **More host tests:** the settings, the thermistor and its limits, the laser, the speed of the spindle, the light and the words of the panel, at their edges.

## [0.0.6] - A save bar that is always there, a backup of the settings, a quicker guard of the spindle and a stream that does not block

- **The task that writes the protect output is watched:** it is subscribed to the task watchdog (set to panic in `sdkconfig.defaults`) and registered with the node guard, so a hang restarts the chip, the pin goes back to input and the stop line opens instead of staying open for good. Not built or run on a board.
- **The node no longer announces itself as the node of the machine:** the shared firmware base is now kept in step by the base tool, which put this node's own name in the default network name, in the Improv device information and in the headers; the network name the set-up creates is `EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD-AUX-<tail of the MAC>`. A host test fails if the other node's name comes back in those places.
- **The spindle is watched every 100 ms again:** the temperature has a task of its own (a DS18B20 conversion blocks for up to 750 ms, and the search for a lost sensor longer), so the speed is judged on its own beat and an overspeed opens the protect output without waiting for the thermometer.
- **Pulses of the speed sensor:** the ring is now a tested class (`core/pulse_ring.hpp`) with unsigned indices (the signed one went negative after about twenty-five days of a spindle at 1 kHz and wrote outside the array), holds a second of pulses at 60000 rpm instead of half a second, and the interrupt takes the lock of the ring. The meter is fed and read under a lock, because the status of the panel and the watch of the spindle both ask for the speed.
- **The camera picture does not hold the web server:** `/stream` is handed to a task of its own (at most two viewers; a third is told to wait), so the status, the measures and the panel answer while somebody looks at the camera.
- **RTSP is limited:** at most three sessions at a time, a session that has not started to play within twenty seconds is dropped, and a session whose picture port is already taken ends instead of ignoring it.
- **The status carries the name of the node** (it was always empty), and the laser's `rejected` counts only the odd readings, not again those the sensor refused.
- **A save bar that is always there:** Save and Save and restart show on every page with settings (off, with a note, until something changes) and Restart is always at hand for an administrator; before, the buttons appeared only after a change and the whole bar was hidden otherwise.
- **Backup of the settings from the panel:** the System page exports every setting to a `.json` file (it holds the Wi-Fi passwords and the token) and imports one back into the forms without sending anything, so each page is saved with its own Save; the id, name and host name of the node are never changed by an import.
- **The backup Wi-Fi networks are saved:** `networkBody()` did not carry `sta.backup`, so adding or removing one showed no change and was never sent. The fields the network pages send are now one list (`NETWORK_FIELDS`) and `tests/check_panel_text.py` fails when a page paints a field that is not in it (or the machine body lacks one it paints).
- **More of the node's settings in the panel:** the Network page edits the name of the node, the time server and its use, the time zone and the periodic restart (the firmware already had them).
- **Texts:** thirteen new texts in the 37 languages. Written for the browser and tried only on a computer, not on a board.

## [0.0.5] - Laser, one-wire, speed sensor and camera picture settings

- **The picture of the camera, set from the panel:** `camera_hmirror`, `camera_vflip`, `camera_brightness`, `camera_contrast`, `camera_saturation` (-2 to 2), `camera_auto_exposure` and, by hand, `camera_exposure` (0 to 1200), and `camera_auto_whitebalance`, applied to the sensor when the camera starts (the OV2640 and the OV5640 take all of them; one that the sensor does not have is left alone). Tested on a computer (`tests/test_aux.cpp`); not tried on a camera yet. The focus of the OV5640 is fixed: its automatic focus needs the firmware of the focus motor that the driver does not load.

- **A speed sensor that tells noise from turns (TCRT5000):** `rpm_input` = `digital` (the output DO on an interrupt, the one to use) or `analog` (the output AO read by the ADC at 20 kHz, `AUX_RPM_ANALOG_GPIO`, with a Schmitt trigger: `rpm_threshold_hi_mv` and `rpm_threshold_lo_mv`, or thresholds that follow the signal with `rpm_auto_threshold`), and the edge that is a pulse (`rpm_edge`, falling for a module that goes low in front of a bright mark). The pulses go through a filter in `core/spindle.hpp`: a pulse closer than the fastest the spindle may turn (`rpm_max`, a quarter above) is noise, a spike at less than 0.4 of the usual gap is refused, a gap of twice the usual one is two turns with a missed pulse; the status shows the quality (`good`, `noisy`, `none`), the pulses refused and, on the analogue input, the level and the thresholds. The ring of pulses holds half a second at 1 kHz. Tested on a computer with simulated trains (`tests/test_rpm.cpp`); not tried on a spindle yet.

- **Several DS18B20 on the same wire:** the search of every sensor (`core/onewire.hpp`: the algorithm of the search of one-wire devices, with the checksum of each address and the families DS18B20, DS1822 and DS18S20), the choice of the spindle's sensor by address (`temp_rom` = `auto` or the 16 hex digits; with several sensors and `auto` the first found is read and the status page says it is a guess), the resolution (`temp_resolution`, 9 to 12 bits, written into the sensor) and a wait that follows the resolution (94 ms at 9 bits, 750 ms at 12). The status of the head lists the sensors found (`temp_roms`), the one in use and the resolution; the panel shows them and has the two settings. Tested on a computer with a simulated bus (`tests/test_onewire.cpp`); not tried on a real wire yet.

- **The laser, set to the job:** the VL53L1X distance mode (`laser_distance_mode`, short or long) and timing budget (`laser_timing_budget_ms`), the receiving area (`laser_roi`, `laser_roi_center`), the filter of outliers (`laser_outlier_k`, `laser_outlier_floor_mm`), the state of every reading (valid, weak signal, high spread, out of range, shown in the panel) and the calibration at a known distance (`POST /api/v1/head/laser/calibrate`, kinds `offset`, `xtalk` and `reset`; stored in the settings of the node, administrators only, and only when `laser_calibration_enabled` is on, which it is not by default). The registers are a plain list in `core/laser_hw.hpp`, tested on a computer with a simulated sensor (`tests/test_laser.cpp`). Not tried on a sensor yet.

## [0.0.4] - The panel in 37 languages

- **The panel in 37 languages:** Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, Estonian, Finnish, Greek, Hungarian, Irish, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Romanian, Slovak, Slovenian, Swedish, Norwegian, Icelandic, Ukrainian, Russian, Serbian, Albanian, Catalan, Galician, Basque, Brazilian Portuguese and Canadian French join the first eight. The words of the new ones are in `panel/locales/CODE.tsv` and are packed into one file, `/langs.js`, that the browser asks for only when one of them is chosen (about 250 KB packed; the panel of the first eight languages is as light as before); a text that a language does not have is shown in English, or in Portuguese or French for the two variants. The node accepts the new codes in `ui.language`. `tests/check_panel_text.py` checks the files (known keys, placeholders, all the texts, the size). The translations are the work of the model and wait for a review by native speakers.

## [0.0.3] - A ring of RGB LEDs around the camera, colour signals and one picture on request

- **A ring of LEDs around the camera:** eight RGB LEDs of the WS2812 kind on one wire (`AUX_LIGHT_GPIO` in the menu of the firmware, the RMT peripheral, no new component). The light has a level from 0 to 100 that follows the eye, a ceiling it never goes over, a tone (white for the camera, cool, warm, red, green, blue) or any colour (`color=RRGGBB`), the sectors that are lit (one side gives a raking light), and a timer that turns a forgotten light off. `GET` and `POST /api/light` and the status carry it; the panel has its settings and four buttons to try it.
- **Signals in colour:** `cue=ok` (green), `nok` (red blinking), `busy` (a blue spot that turns), `warn` (amber) and `alarm` (fast red) show for a while on top of the light, so the head says how a check went without anyone looking at a screen.
- **One picture on request:** `GET /api/snapshot` gives the camera as JPEG (with the token of the head), for the programs that check the work.
- **Tests:** `test_light` (231 checks): the settings, the curve of the levels, the ceiling, the sectors, the requests and what is wrong with them, the colours and the signals, the timer and the words of the status.

## [0.0.2] - The web panel and the update from GitHub of the other nodes, on their firmware base

- **The firmware base of EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD and its web panel:** the node no longer reads `head.cfg` from its own partition. It has the settings document in flash, the users and the setup access point, the Wi-Fi with up to three backup networks, the Ethernet of the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ETH or the Wi-Fi of an ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (the `s3-eth` and `s3-wifi` board profiles), the clock, the guard of the tasks and the memory, Improv Wi-Fi over the USB cable and the rollback of a firmware that does not come up. What is only the head's (the camera, the streams, the laser, the sensors of the spindle, the protect output) is in `main/head.cpp` and in the `head` section of the settings.
- **The panel of the other nodes:** the menu in groups with the language and the sign-out at its foot, a Summary, a Wi-Fi page with the backup networks, a Head page that edits the settings of the camera, the streams, the laser and the spindle (the passwords and the token are write-only), Help, About, users, the log and the system page; the status page shows the camera, the laser, the speed, the temperature and the protect output and takes one measure of the table on request. Eight languages in every text, checked by a test.
- **Update from GitHub and copy of a firmware slot:** the node looks for the newest release of its own repository, takes the image of its board (`eh3d-node-bungard-aux-<version>-<board>-app.bin`), checks the SHA-256 that the release publishes and installs it into the other slot with the rollback of the boot loader; the whole firmware of one slot can be copied over the other in both directions. The releases are not signed.
- **The camera needs a signed-in person or the token:** the picture and the readings are served to a program with the access token of the head and to a person signed in to the panel without it; the status route stays readable by the token only.
- **Tests:** the settings document of the head (read, checked, written with the secrets kept out), the update from GitHub, the clock, what the nodes share on both board profiles, the texts of the panel in eight languages and the stacks of the tasks.

- **The firmware builds:** with ESP-IDF 5.5.5 for the ESP32-S3 (608 KB, 81% of the application partition free), with the camera driver, the VL53L1X component and the W5500 of the board; it has not been flashed or run.

- **Through the server:** the node is reached by EH3D-SERVER too (`/api/v1/machines/:id/head/...`), with the address and the token of the node in the machine's configuration.

- **Two cameras, the OV2640 and the OV5640:** `core/camera.hpp` lists the sizes each sensor can give; the sensor is asked which one it is (the product id), the node starts the camera at the largest size that `camera_width` and `camera_height` allow and that sensor can do, reports `camera_model` on `/api/status`, and the ONVIF encoder options list only what that sensor gives. `camera_model = auto | ov2640 | ov5640` in `head.cfg`; the pins of the camera connector of the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ETH are the defaults of `menuconfig`.
- **The drivers of the sensors:** the VL53L1X laser through the component `grrtzm/vl53l1x_library`, and three ways to read the temperature of the spindle: a DS18B20 on one wire, an NTC thermistor on an analogue input (`ntc_series_ohm`, `ntc_r25_ohm`, `ntc_beta`) and an MLX90614 infrared thermometer on the I2C bus. The checksums and the conversions (`core/thermo.hpp`) are tested on a computer, with the published check values of both CRCs; the drivers have never run on a board.
- **More tests:** 203 checks in the two host programs.

## [0.0.1] - The rules of the node: the settings file, the measure of the laser, the watch of the spindle and the words of the network

- **The settings file:** `head.cfg` holds every tuned value (the network, the camera, the laser, the spindle, the temperature, the protect output); a value out of range or an unknown key is reported and the safe default stays, and limits that make no sense together fall back to the defaults.
- **The measure of the table:** several laser readings become one distance: the odd ones are thrown away (median and median absolute deviation), the range and the offset are applied, and a measure that spreads too much, lies out of the range or has too few readings is refused with the reason.
- **The spindle:** the speed from the pulses of a sensor (any number per turn, zero when the pulses stop), the temperature with a warning, a critical level and a hysteresis, and the protect output that opens when the spindle is too fast or too hot or when a sensor that is on does not answer.
- **The words of the network:** `GET /api/status`, `POST /api/measure` and `GET /stream`, the token in the header or the query compared in constant time, the answers as JSON and the line `Z x y height` that the desktop programs read as a point of the map of the table.
- **The firmware, built but never flashed:** the ESP-IDF project for an ESP32-S3 with a W5500 network chip (the network, the web server, the camera, the pulses of the spindle and the protect output); the drivers of the laser and of the temperature sensors are not written.
- **The video as an IP camera sends it:** RTSP (OPTIONS, DESCRIBE, SETUP, PLAY, PAUSE, GET_PARAMETER, TEARDOWN; JPEG over RTP over UDP or interleaved in the TCP connection; Digest and Basic login) and ONVIF (WS-Discovery, and the SOAP services of profile S: capabilities, services, profiles, video sources and encoders with the sizes the camera can do, the address of the stream and of the snapshot, the user token), with the user and the password of the settings; without a password nothing is served. The firmware that carries them is not built.
- **Host tests:** two programs with 175 checks on the rules above and on the video (the hashes of the logins against their published vectors, the RTSP session, the RTP payload of JPEG and ONVIF).
