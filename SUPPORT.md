# Support

* **Questions and ideas:** open an issue with the form that fits.
* **A measure is refused or the protect output stays open:** say what the log prints at start-up (the lines about `head.cfg`), the answer of `/api/status` and which sensors are switched on in the settings.
* **Hardware questions:** see `docs/HARDWARE.md` first. If you have the schematic of your board or a capture of its pins, that is the most useful thing you can share.

This is a one-person project and the node is at its first steps: answers can take a few days.
